Pedestrian detection in Hong Kong street-level imagery

Non-commercial academic research affiliated with the Department of Geography, Hong Kong Baptist University.

This site identifies the research application used to access Lands Department Streetscape 360 imagery. About 9,000 panoramas will be sampled across Hong Kong’s 18 districts, on average about 500 per district, about every 50 metres along pedestrian streets. The Lands Department will be acknowledged as the data source.
